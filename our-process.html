Common Questions

What to expect, before you start.

Do I need to already own land?

No. Many clients come to us before they've found a lot. Land evaluation against real comp data is the first stage of our process, not a prerequisite for starting a conversation.

How long does a custom build typically take?

Land to close generally runs several months to about a year, depending on permitting timelines, site conditions, and the scope of the home. We'll give you a realistic estimate specific to your project once we understand it.

What if I'm a real estate company, not a homeowner?

We work with both. If you're a real estate company or developer looking for a contractor to build homes for you, the process starts the same way, a conversation about scope, timeline, and target areas.

How involved will I be during construction?

As involved as you want to be. Weekly cost-to-complete tracking means you always know where the project stands, whether you check in daily or prefer periodic updates.
