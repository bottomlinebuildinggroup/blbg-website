Built on a simple idea: run every dollar like it's yours.

Grounded in Colossians 3:17 and 3:23, working at everything as an act of service, not just a job, our mission is to deliver luxury custom homes with the same discipline, communication, and craftsmanship that turns a stressful build into one you actually enjoy.

Bottom Line Building Group was founded by Jeremy Bottoms to bring that same disciplined, client-first, faith-driven approach to ground-up luxury custom homes across Charlotte Metro.

The goal is straightforward, a distinctive home, delivered on budget and on plan, built by a team that treats your project's bottom line the way we'd treat our own.
